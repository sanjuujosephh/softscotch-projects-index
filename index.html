1. softscotch

Type: agency. Not software. The human layer: people who handle search, AI answer visibility, ad campaigns on Google and Meta, messaging over email, SMS and WhatsApp, automation, and website and content builds.
Home: https://softscotch.com

2. postinstantly

Type: web app plus browser extension.
Modules: ideas board, three-question drafting, editor (30+ tools), multi-network calendar, repurposing, carousel, thread and infographic makers, bulk creator (up to 14 days), voice to post.
Targets: LinkedIn, X, Threads, Instagram.
Home: PostInstantly

3. darnitseo

Type: web app.
Modules: audit engine (75+ checks), ranked fix list, AI prompt tracking, local rank grid, competitor view, citation checks, Google Business Profile audit.
Integrations: Google Search Console. Publishing to WordPress, Ghost, Shopify and Webflow.
Extras: 100+ standalone tools, no signup needed.
Home: DarnItSEO

4. brandsforcreators

Type: two-sided web app (brands and US creators).
Modules: listings (spots, deadline, deliverables), applications, trust score (0 to 100), natural-language search, tag scan (TikTok, Instagram), outreach sequences with reply detection and a deliverability score, CRM, shared inbox, content library.
Home: BrandsForCreators

5. marketingnewsfeed

Type: news aggregator.
Inputs: 30+ sources. Each item gets priority, summary, category and update type.
Outputs: feed, action list, platform status, glossary, critical email alerts.
Home: Marketing Newsfeed

6. verifiedwidget

Type: embeddable script.
Input: a Google Maps link. No API key.
Output: one of 9 layouts, about 12 KB, async, rendered without an iframe, plus JSON-LD for 14 business types.
Config: domain allowlist, multi-location.
Home: VerifiedWidget

7. amlookingfor

Type: content hub, US focused.
Sections: People, Launches, Directories, Ranked.
Home: amlookingfor
